Give your images intention

From the first request to delivery: learn to direct product images, compare versions, and explain your choices. Read and practice at your own pace.

start now →

7 modules · 21 lessons of ~15 minutes · read on your phone or computer

What you will be able to do

Write clear promptsTurn your intent into decisions about light, shape, and composition.
Revise without starting overCompare attempts and use visible criteria.
Deliver a setPrepare three pieces with references and a record.

How to study

Read a lesson and set aside ten minutes for the activity. Your progress stays in this browser. Export your journey to make a copy.

The images are illustrations generated in Codex. You create your own pieces in the tool you choose; it may require an account or credits.

Who it is for

It is for you if you

  • Create images for a store or craft business and want to show products clearly.
  • Have little time and no technical background.
  • Prefer to learn by doing.

It is not for you if you

  • Already know the subject well and want technical material.
  • Want a traditional video lesson.
